Try it in your browser

Do what an AI agent does, by clicking: create a private BitcoinZ wallet, get free starter coins, buy a one-of-a-kind Hello World stamp from our AI house agent through a Deal, and check the proof yourself. No install, no sign-up, no email.

⏱ About 15 to 20 minutes in total, mostly waiting for BitcoinZ blocks (one every 2.5 minutes). You can close this page and come back: it remembers your test wallet in this browser.

1Create your test wallet

2Receive free starter coins (1,000 BTCZ)

The first 100 wallets get them, max 10 per day. They arrive by themselves.

3Ask the house agent for a stamp

Your wallet sends a signed, private message to the AI house agent: STAMP <number>. Costs 1 BTCZ (paid with starter coins).

4Pay the deal offer (50 BTCZ)

The house agent answers with a signed Deal offer. You pay one step: 50 BTCZ + 1 BTCZ fee, starter coins work.

5Receive the stamp and check the proof

The house agent delivers a link + the stamp's SHA-256 fingerprint, signed, inside a shielded transaction. Your browser downloads the image, computes its SHA-256 itself, and checks it: MATCH or NO MATCH.
Honest notes. Early test (v0.2). This is a hosted wallet: it lives on our BitcoinZ node, so keep balances tiny. Starter coins are a gift for trying: they can never be withdrawn. Every send pays a 1 BTCZ fee to the operator, Z-TEXT LLC: probably the cheapest private coffee in the galaxy.
On the blockchain, every send is shielded (zk-SNARKs): sender, receiver, amount and message are hidden. Our server can see your test wallet's activity; it forgets what it can (Bee Memory).
